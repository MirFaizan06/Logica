# Code of Conduct

Logica is a learning project, and people learn best where they feel safe asking questions and being wrong. This code of conduct applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to any space where someone represents the project.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. People contribute for different reasons and at different levels.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Logic is a good place for arguments, and bad arguments should be answered with better ones.
- Be specific and constructive in reviews. Say what is wrong and, where you can, how to fix it.
- Respect that contributors have limited time. A slow reply is not a refusal.
- Accept correction gracefully, whether you are giving it or receiving it.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or demeaning comments, including about someone's background, identity, age, ability or experience.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Anything else that would reasonably make someone feel unwelcome here.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules. They will try to explain why when they do.

To report a problem, email techbytesdesign@gmail.com. Reports are read by the maintainers and handled privately. If the report is about a maintainer, say so in the email and it will be handled by someone else where possible.
